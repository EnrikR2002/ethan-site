# Visitor fit and self-rejection audit

Date: October 4, 2026

## Conclusion

The active funnel repeatedly implied that an existing audience, a diagnosed growth problem, a clear brief and an implementation team were prerequisites to talking with Ethan. Those requirements confused the fit for one product (the Growth Bottleneck Sprint) with Ethan's broader launch, content and community work.

The revised copy invites visitors to discuss their stage and goals. The Sprint still requires a useful journey and real audience/customer signal; launch work and implementation remain separately scoped. Pricing is unchanged. These are editorial risks identified in the copy, not measured abandonment or conversion findings.

## Scope boundaries that remain useful

- The Sprint is $2,500 over two weeks with four defined deliverables.
- It investigates one journey rather than running an entire marketing department.
- Evidence is needed for a meaningful diagnosis; its adequacy is assessed together before commitment.
- Implementation is optional and priced separately; development, specialist design and media buying may need another specialist.
- Launch work has no invented package price or guaranteed audience outcome.
- Historical results remain attributed to their projects; these do not become guarantees for new clients.

## Other surfaces reviewed

The resume wrapper and source PDF do not state a minimum audience, revenue, team size or geographic requirement. The approved Objective uses "ambitious brands"; that exact owner-supplied copy is preserved, with broader invitation language elsewhere.

The approved Sprint PDF is accurately specific to its product, including its existing-signal opening. Its content and preview image remain unchanged; the web wrapper now provides broader consulting context. Someone receiving only the downloaded PDF will still see the Sprint's narrower positioning, so share the profile/consulting URL for a general introduction.

The original consulting archive remains preserved and noindexed. It is outside the active navigation and retains an old 15-minute intro label and general Calendly URL. That is a legacy inconsistency, not an active funnel qualification. A future archive notice could point to current consulting without rewriting the preserved content.

The Sprint PDF's printed booking button has no embedded booking hyperlink. The offer web page has working Fit Call links above and below the preview. A future PDF link-only update would improve downloaded-document navigation without changing the product.

The automatic resume prompt appears at the profile footer and covers the consulting invitation on a phone. Its close and "Keep exploring" controls work. The owner previously requested the popup, so this audit preserves it; an inline or manually opened resume invitation would reduce that interruption if the owner chooses to change it.

Calendly's actual event is "20 Minute Consulting", lasts 20 minutes and reaches an attendee form with required name/email and an optional preparation note. The inspected form has no budget, follower-count, team-size or business-stage screening question. No details were entered and no appointment was submitted.

## Verification and limits

- Production build and whitespace checks pass for the revised pages.
- Review includes the profile, SF Tech Week profile, consulting, case studies, offer wrapper, resume wrapper, both full one-page PDFs and the preserved archive.
- Mobile and desktop browser checks cover the changed invitations, fit section, FAQ and offer context before publication.
- The booking destination remains the existing direct Calendly event; no appointment was submitted during this audit.
- No visitor conversion lift or actual abandonment is claimed. Compare observed paths after analytics storage is activated.

## Copy rule for future edits

Invite the conversation using the visitor's stage and goal. Define product fit after that. Express budget, evidence and execution requirements in the context of the particular engagement, rather than as blanket prerequisites for getting help.
